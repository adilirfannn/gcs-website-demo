Delivery & installation

Last updated: Draft, November 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver to Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Multan, Sialkot and Gujranwala, where GCS has offices and technicians. Other cities can be arranged on request.

When your order arrives

After you place an order, our team calls you to confirm the details, your address and a delivery time. Nothing is dispatched until the order is confirmed.

Installation of copiers

Photocopiers and multifunction machines are delivered and installed by GCS technicians, who connect the machine to your network and show your staff how to use it. Please make sure there is a suitable power point and space for the machine.

Charges

Any delivery or installation charge is told to you on the confirmation call, before dispatch, and appears on your invoice.
