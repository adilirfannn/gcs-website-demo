How it works

Print anywhere. Release securely. Track everything.

  1. 1 Send Users print from any laptop, phone or Chromebook to a single secure queue. No hunting for the right driver.
  2. 2 Authenticate At any printer, they tap an ID card or enter a PIN. Nothing prints until the owner is standing there.
  3. 3 Release & track The job prints, and every page is logged against the user, department and cost centre.
