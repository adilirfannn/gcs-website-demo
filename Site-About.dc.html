About GCS

A pioneer of document
solutions in Pakistan.

We supply and maintain new and refurbished heavy, medium and light duty photocopiers and printers, backed by after-sales service, for multinational and national companies and foreign and local banks.

Our story

Three decades of growth

  1. 1995 Founded

    Established in August 1995 as a small business unit selling office automation machines.

  2. 2003 New leadership

    Management taken over by a team of experienced professionals, with offices in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Sialkot and Multan.

  3. 2016 Private Limited

    Incorporated as a Private Limited Company with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan.

  4. Today PaperCut & online shop

    A PaperCut Certified Authorized Solution Center serving corporates and banks nationwide, now also selling machines through our online shop.

More than machines

We run our own import setup for the latest photocopiers and printers in bulk, to meet the demand of Pakistan's corporate sector. Our document solutions are also offered under the Document Service Plan (DSP) and Document Assurance Plan (DAP).

Beyond machine rental, we provide internet-based wide-format CAD/CAM engineering copier solutions, with full support for machine monitoring, PaperCut and scanning software. We also digitise documents to preserve an organisation's historical records.
